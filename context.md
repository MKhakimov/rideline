# RIDELINE — Project Context

## Overview

RIDELINE is a community-driven outdoor platform focused on mountain biking, hiking, outdoor adventures and exploring Uzbekistan.

The platform allows users to discover trips, explore routes, view photography and read articles about outdoor activities.

## Audience

Outdoor enthusiasts, mountain bikers, hikers and local adventure communities.

## Geography

Primary focus: Uzbekistan.

## Content

- Trip reports and photo galleries
- Mountain routes and GPX tracks
- Articles and guides
- Videos (planned)
- Community resources and Telegram groups/channels

## Content Management

All content is added manually. No user-generated content system is planned initially.

## Languages

Russian (primary) and English.

## Design Direction

- Minimalist and refined UI
- Typography-focused design
- Clean layouts with generous whitespace
- Subtle animations
- No unnecessary decorative elements
- Responsive design

## Development Principles

- Prioritize performance, accessibility and SEO.
- Use reusable, maintainable components.
- Keep architecture simple and scalable.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Prefer native CSS/SCSS over utility-first frameworks.
- Use TypeScript and strict typing.
